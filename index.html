Who We Are

A place to grow in faith and purpose

Refiners Chapel Katoogo exists to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, nurture believers, and help people grow into the purpose God has for their lives.

We seek to encourage righteous living and build communities grounded in the Word of God, prayer, holiness and truth.

Our Vision

Make Christ Known

To boldly proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, shining the light of His truth into our community and beyond, that all may come to know Him as Lord and Savior.

2 Corinthians 4:5

Our Mission

Transform Lives, Align Destinies

To refine lives through the Word of God and prayer, nurturing believers to be vessels of honor—transformed by Christ and aligned to their God-given purpose.

Malachi 3:3; Jeremiah 1:5

Our Commitment

We encourage righteous living and seek to restore and build communities grounded in holiness and truth.
