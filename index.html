About Us

Refiners Chapel Katoogo exists to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, nurture believers, and help people grow into the purpose God has for their lives.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

Make Christ Known

Boldly proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ and shine the light of His truth in our community and beyond, that all may know Him as Lord and Savior.

Scripture: 2 Corinthians 4:5

Our Mission

Transform Lives, Align Destinies

Refine lives through the Word of God and prayer, nurture believers as vessels of honor, and help them be transformed by Christ and aligned to their God-given purpose.

Scriptures: Malachi 3:3; Jeremiah 1:5

What We Are Committed To

We encourage righteous living and seek to restore and build communities grounded in holiness and truth.
