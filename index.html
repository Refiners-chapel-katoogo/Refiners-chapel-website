Who We Are

A place to grow in faith and purpose

Refiners Chapel Katoogo is a Christ-centred ministry based in Uganda, committed to proclaiming the Gospel of Jesus Christ, nurturing believers and helping people grow into their God-given purpose.

With a heart for our local community and a welcoming outlook to the world, we seek to build lives and communities grounded in the Word of God, prayer, holiness and truth.

Our Direction

Vision & Mission

What guides our calling, our service and our commitment to the community.

01

Our Vision

Make Christ Known

To boldly proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, shining the light of His truth into our community and beyond, that all may come to know Him as Lord and Savior.

2 Corinthians 4:5
02

Our Mission

Transform Lives, Align Destinies

To refine lives through the Word of God and prayer, nurturing believers to be vessels of honor—transformed by Christ and aligned to their God-given purpose.

Malachi 3:3; Jeremiah 1:5
03

Our Commitment

We encourage righteous living and seek to restore and build communities grounded in holiness and truth.
