Your information and privacy

Privacy Policy

Refiners Chapel Katoogo respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected through this website, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the website, information may include details you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, telephone number, message, event or media submission, and photographs or videos. Technical information may also be processed by the website host and service providers, such as browser type, device information, IP address, and basic access logs.

2. How we use information

We may use submitted information to respond to enquiries, communicate church news and activities, receive and review media or announcement submissions, publish approved church content, maintain website security, and improve the website. We will not use your contact details for unrelated purposes without an appropriate basis or permission.

3. Photos, videos and information about others

Please only upload or send photographs, videos or personal details that you are authorised to share. Obtain permission from identifiable people where appropriate, and take particular care with images or information about children. Contact us if you believe material has been published without appropriate permission.

4. When information is shared

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the website, including GitHub Pages for hosting and Supabase for certain website data and media storage. If you contact us through WhatsApp or email, those services will also process information under their own policies. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect people, the church or the website.

5. Public content

Content approved for publication, including announcements, photographs or videos, may be visible to anyone who visits the website and may be copied or shared by others. Do not submit information that you expect to remain private for public posting.

6. Storage and security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. No website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Access to administrative areas should be limited to authorised church personnel. Information may be stored on systems located outside Uganda, depending on the service providers used.

7. How long information is kept

We keep information for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, to maintain church records, or to meet legal obligations. Published material may remain online until it is removed or replaced. You can contact us to request review, correction or removal, subject to applicable requirements.

8. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask what personal information you have provided, request correction of inaccurate information, ask about deletion or withdrawal of permission, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity and may not be able to fulfil every request where a legal or legitimate record-keeping requirement applies.

9. Children and young people

This website is intended for general church information and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate involvement or permission from a parent, guardian or responsible adult. Please contact us if you believe a child’s information has been submitted inappropriately.

10. Cookies and external links

The website may use essential technical features and its hosting or embedded services may use cookies or similar technologies. Links to third-party websites and services are governed by their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies before sharing information with them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as website features or our practices change. The latest version will be available on this page.

12. Contact the church

For privacy questions or requests, contact Refiners Chapel Katoogo at refinerschapeluganda@gmail.com or WhatsApp +256 751 088849.

Last updated: 20 November 2026. This policy is intended as a practical website privacy notice and should be reviewed by the church against its actual data practices and applicable Ugandan privacy law.
